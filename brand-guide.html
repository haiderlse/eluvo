How Eluvo looks and sounds. For designers, developers and anyone posting on Eluvo's behalf. Version 2 moves the brand to purple and yellow with the three-bar mark; colours and type below are drawn from it.

1 · Logo

One mark, three parts

The symbol is three rising bars: a short yellow one, then purple, then ink, each taller than the last, like steps climbed one at a time. The wordmark is lowercase "eluvo" in Outfit 800. The tagline sits below or beside the lock-up, never inside it.

eluvo

Primary: full logo on cream. Default everywhere.

eluvo

On white: cards, documents, email.

eluvo

On purple: reversed. White wordmark, yellow bar stays, the other two go lilac and white.

Clear space and size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "e" in the wordmark. Never smaller than 90 px wide on screen or 25 mm in print. Below that, use the symbol alone.

App icon and favicon

Symbol only, centred, on cream. Files: favicon.png (32) and icon-192.png in the site root, both made from logo/eluvo-symbol.svg.

Do

  • Use the files in the logo folder as they are.
  • Put the logo top-left on screens, top-centre on documents.
  • Pair it with plenty of cream or white.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects.
  • Put the full logo on photos, patterns or yellow.
  • Write "Eluvo" in another font next to the mark.
  • Use the tagline as a heading on every screen.

2 · Colour

Purple carries, yellow acts, amber points

The brand colours come from the logo. Purple is the voice of the brand: the hero, dark surfaces, secondary buttons, with ink for text. Yellow is the one action colour: primary buttons, the thing to press. Amber is yellow darkened until it reads on cream, for small pointers: labels, links, the tagline. Cream is the ground. Green is kept for "verified" and "safe" only, so it always means something.

Plum#3B2E6B

Hero, dark sections, secondary buttons.

Ink#1F1A3A

Text, headings, darkest surfaces.

Yellow#F4B205

Primary buttons with ink text. Highlights.

Amber#8A6100

Labels, links, tagline on light grounds.

Cream#FBF9F5

Page background. Never pure white pages.

Slate#5A5670

Secondary text, captions, metadata.

Fern#2F7D5B

Verified badge, success. Not decoration.

Signal red#C0392B

Crisis banner and errors only.

Contrast rules

Ink on cream and ink on yellow (8.9:1) both pass for body text. White on yellow fails at 1.9:1; never put white text on a yellow button. Yellow text works on plum (6.3:1) but never on cream or white; use amber there (5.3:1). Slate is for text 14 px and up.

3 · Type

Outfit for headings, Figtree for everything else

Outfit is a soft geometric that sits naturally next to the rounded wordmark. Figtree is quiet and very readable at small sizes, which matters in a chat. Both are free on Google Fonts, licensed for web and apps.

Case

Sentence case everywhere, including buttons. Uppercase only for small labels, with letter-spacing.

Numbers

Tabular figures for prices, ratings and confidence scores so columns line up.

4 · Tagline

"Grow forward" is a signature, not a slogan

Where it goes

  • Next to or under the logo in the lock-up.
  • App store subtitle. Email signature. Last line of a deck.
  • Social bios.

Where it doesn't

  • Not as a page heading or a button.
  • Not inside AI replies to students.
  • Not reworded ("grow forwards", "growing forward").

5 · Voice

Like a text from someone who listens

Eluvo talks to people who are nervous, tired of rejection, or unsure what they want. Short sentences. Warm, not bubbly. Specific, not inspirational. We say what happens next.

We say

  • "What's going on this week?"
  • "You already have skills worth talking about."
  • "One next step for today."
  • "Coaching, not therapy."
  • "Book a free 30-minute call."

We don't say

  • "Unlock your potential", "empower", "journey", "transform your life".
  • "Don't be nervous."
  • "Our revolutionary AI."
  • Anything that promises a job or a result.
  • Exclamation marks, more than one per screen.
Headline: What's going on this week?
Button: Talk to Coach Kim · free

Error: That didn't send. Check your connection and try again.
