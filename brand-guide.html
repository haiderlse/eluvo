Brand guide · v1 · November 2026

Eluvo

Grow forward

How Eluvo looks and sounds. For designers, developers and anyone posting on Eluvo's behalf. The logo is the one the founders chose; colours and type below are drawn from it.

1 · Logo

One mark, three parts

The symbol is a V of three strokes, navy, orange and gold, like leaves opening or a path splitting upward. The wordmark is lowercase "eluvo". The tagline sits to the right of the wordmark or below the mark, never inside it.

Primary: full logo on cream. Default everywhere.

On white: cards, documents, email.

On navy: symbol only until the designer supplies a reversed (white) wordmark.

Clear space and size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "e" in the wordmark. Never smaller than 120 px wide on screen or 30 mm in print. Below that, use the symbol alone.

App icon and favicon

Symbol only, centred, on cream or navy. Files: eluvo_symbol_512 / 192 / 64 / 32 and eluvo_app_icon_cream_512, eluvo_app_icon_navy_512.

Do

  • Use the files in 01_Brand/Logo as they are.
  • Put the logo top-left on screens, top-centre on documents.
  • Pair it with plenty of cream or white.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects.
  • Put the full logo on photos, patterns or orange.
  • Write "Eluvo" in another font next to the mark.
  • Use the tagline as a heading on every screen.

2 · Colour

Navy carries, gold acts, orange points

All five brand colours come from the logo. Navy is the voice of the brand: headings, body text, dark surfaces. Gold is the one action colour: primary buttons, the thing to press. Orange is for small pointers: labels, links, the tagline. Cream is the ground. Green is kept for "verified" and "safe" only, so it always means something.

Navy#182B4D

Text, headings, nav, dark sections.

Deep navy#0D1F3B

Footer, app icon ground, chat header.

Gold#CC8F40

Primary buttons with navy text. Highlights.

Orange#A45627

Labels, links, tagline, small accents.

Cream#F6F4F0

Page background. Never pure white pages.

Slate#545E71

Secondary text, captions, metadata.

Fern#2F7D5B

Verified badge, success. Not decoration.

Signal red#C0392B

Crisis banner and errors only.

Contrast rules

Navy on cream and navy on gold both pass for body text. White on gold does not; never put white text on a gold button. White on orange passes only at 18 px bold or larger, so orange is not a button colour. Slate is for text 14 px and up.

3 · Type

Outfit for headings, Figtree for everything else

Outfit is a soft geometric that sits naturally next to the rounded wordmark. Figtree is quiet and very readable at small sizes, which matters in a chat. Both are free on Google Fonts, licensed for web and apps.

Scale

12 · 14.5 · 17 · 19 · 28 · 44 · 60. Stay on it. Line height 1.1 for display, 1.55 for body.

Case

Sentence case everywhere, including buttons. Uppercase only for small labels, with letter-spacing.

Numbers

Tabular figures for prices, ratings and confidence scores so columns line up.

4 · Tagline

"Grow forward" is a signature, not a slogan

Where it goes

  • Next to or under the logo in the lock-up.
  • App store subtitle. Email signature. Last line of a deck.
  • Social bios.

Where it doesn't

  • Not as a page heading or a button.
  • Not inside AI replies to students.
  • Not reworded ("grow forwards", "growing forward").

5 · Voice

Like a text from someone who listens

Eluvo talks to people who are nervous, tired of rejection, or unsure what they want. Short sentences. Warm, not bubbly. Specific, not inspirational. We say what happens next.

We say

  • "What's going on this week?"
  • "You already have skills worth talking about."
  • "One next step for today."
  • "Coaching, not therapy."
  • "Book a free 30-minute call."

We don't say

  • "Unlock your potential", "empower", "journey", "transform your life".
  • "Don't be nervous."
  • "Our revolutionary AI."
  • Anything that promises a job or a result.
  • Exclamation marks, more than one per screen.
Headline: Find a coach for your next step.
Button: Talk to Coach Kim · free
Empty state: No coaches match yet. Try fewer filters, or tell us what you need and we'll find one.
Error: That didn't send. Check your connection and try again.
Crisis banner: It sounds like things are really heavy right now. You deserve support from a person today. Lifeline 13 11 14, or 000 if you are in danger.
