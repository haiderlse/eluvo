How it works

Name it first. Then the help makes sense.

1

Say what is going on

In your own words, typed or spoken. No sign-up, any hour. You don’t need to know what the problem is.

2

Find what is in the way

Three quick questions, then a name for it: fear of another no, not seeing what you offer, too much at once. You say whether it fits.

3

One next step, and help that fits

A step small enough for today, practice with the AI coach, and a real coach when you want a person. Come back and it asks how it went.

You do not need to have your entire future figured out. You just need to know what to do next.
