How it works

Three steps, the first two are free

1

Say what is going on

Type it in your own words. A coach's AI answers straight away, any hour, with no sign-up.

2

Practise and watch it move

Build one interview answer at midnight. Rate your confidence before and after, and see it climb over the week. One next step each time.

3

Book the human when it matters

The AI knows when to hand over. A free discovery call, then paid sessions on the coach's own terms.

Note 6 · The coach sideTwo thousand coaches will only join if setup takes an hour and they keep control. The four steps here are the onboarding. The "what you keep" box is the answer to "will the AI replace me".

For coaches

Coach 500 people without working more hours

Train your AI coach in about an hour. It does the daily practice in your voice. You do the sessions.

1Upload two or three recorded sessions, or write out how a typical one goes.
2Answer 20 questions: your go-to questions, how you open, how you close, what you never say.
3Read 10 sample replies from your AI and correct them. That becomes its rulebook.
4Set what is free, what is paid, and when the AI must hand over to you.

What you keep

  • Your playbook. It is yours, and it leaves with you.
  • Your clients. Every conversation is visible to you.
  • The off switch. Pause your AI coach any time.
  • Your price. Eluvo takes a small cut of bookings, nothing else.
  • Each other. Referrals, shared worksheets, peer circles.

About this mockup

    Name: Eluvo. "Grow forward." The three-bar logo sets the palette: purple #3B2E6B, ink #1F1A3A, yellow #F4B205 on cream #FBF9F5. Fonts Outfit and Figtree. Full rules in the brand guide.
  2. 2
    Conversation first. The hero starts a chat instead of a search. Quick chips use the words trainees actually say; Kim's posts gave us those words.
  3. 3
    Directory like Oladoc. Filters left, cards right, free action and paid action on every card, verified badge, price in the same spot.
  4. 4
    Only Kim is real. Her credentials come from LinkedIn (ICI Advanced Practitioner, IAP Certified Career Coach, MBA, ex Country Head of HR, Telstra career coach). The other six coaches are invented examples.
  5. 5
    Coach Kim AI is live when opened in Claude. Press "Talk to Coach Kim". The first message asks your permission, then it answers using a rulebook built from her method: start where you are, name the feeling, find the strength, STAR answers one at a time, rate confidence 1 to 10, end with one next step. Outside Claude it falls back to demo replies.
  6. 6
    Coaches join in an hour and keep their playbook, clients, price and the off switch.
  7. 7
    Guides and videos, by situation. One video, one guide, one tool for each of five situations, each ending in practice with the AI coach. Videos are placeholders; guides are drafts for Kim to edit.
  8. 8
    Confidence is saved. Each 1 to 10 rating in the chat is kept on the device and shown as rising bars, in the chat and in "Start here". It is the reason to come back, and it can be cleared in one tap.
