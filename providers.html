Why start there

Same symptom, three different causes

Three young people on your caseload have stopped applying.

One expects another no

Four rejections in, not applying feels safer. What helps: one small, low-stakes attempt.

One can’t see what they offer

Thinks no experience means nothing to say. What helps: naming three strengths from real moments.

One is carrying things at home

Money and family are using up the energy. What helps: a person, not a worksheet.

The same resume workshop helps none of them. Eluvo helps each one name what is in the way, so the support that follows fits.

What your participants get

Self-awareness first. Then confidence and follow-through.

For the moments that decide things: job searching, rejection, interviews and the first weeks in work. Nothing to install; they open a link on their phone.

A name for what’s in the way

A two-minute check in their own words. They say whether it fits. Most have never been asked.

One next step they chose

Small enough for today, written by them, and followed up when they come back.

Practice that fits

Answers out loud for nerves, strengths for self-doubt, one application for avoidance. Any hour.

A person when it matters

Handover to a verified human coach, or back to your consultant, when someone needs more than practice.

You see

  • What looks to be in the way, when the participant chooses to share it.
  • Who has used it, and when.
  • Confidence ratings and next steps done over time.
  • When someone asks for a person.

You don’t see

  • What anyone typed, or what the AI said back.
  • Anything from a participant who has not agreed to share.
  • Any score or ranking of a person.

Running a pilot

One cohort, eight weeks, three steps

1

Pick a cohort

One team or one intake, 20 to 50 people. We agree what you want to learn before it starts.

2

Share a link

Consultants hand over a link or QR code in an appointment. Participants choose whether to share their progress with you.

3

Review together

At eight weeks we go through what was in the way, what moved, and what consultants noticed. You decide whether to continue.

For employment providers

Fits around appointments and job plans. Useful for interview preparation, first jobs and the weeks after a knock-back.

For universities and TAFEs

Extends a careers service that cannot see every student. Covers graduate applications, interviews and exam nerves.

Safety built in

Coaching, not therapy. The AI is always labelled as AI. A crisis message stops the coaching and shows Lifeline 13 11 14 and 000.
