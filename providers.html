For employment providers and universities

A coach for every young person, between appointments.

Your consultants see each person for a short time. The hard moments come later: the night before an interview, the hour after a rejection. Eluvo is there for those, and tells you who needs a person.

The interview is on Thursday. The nerves arrive on Wednesday night. That is the gap Eluvo covers.

What your participants get

Help at the moment they need it

Nothing to install. They open a link on their phone and start talking.

An AI coach, any hour

Built on a real coach's method. It asks one question at a time and ends every chat with one next step.

Practice, not reading

They build one interview answer from a real moment, then say it back. Short videos and plans for the week they are having.

Confidence they can see

A 1 to 10 rating before and after each practice. Small movement, made visible.

A person when it matters

The AI hands over to a verified human coach, or back to your consultant, when someone needs more than practice.

You see

  • Who has used it, and when.
  • How many practice sessions, on which topics.
  • Confidence ratings over time.
  • When someone asks for a person.

You don't see

  • What anyone typed, or what the AI said back.
  • Anything from a participant who has not agreed to share.
  • Any score or ranking of a person.

Running a pilot

One cohort, eight weeks, three steps

1

Pick a cohort

One team or one intake, 20 to 50 people. We agree what you want to learn before it starts.

2

Share a link

Consultants hand over a link or QR code in an appointment. Participants choose whether to share their progress with you.

3

Review together

At eight weeks we go through usage, confidence change and what consultants noticed, and you decide whether to continue.

For employment providers

Fits around appointments and job plans. Useful for interview preparation, first jobs and the weeks after a knock-back.

For universities and TAFEs

Extends a careers service that cannot see every student. Covers graduate applications, interviews and exam nerves.

Safety built in

Coaching, not therapy. The AI is always labelled as AI. A crisis message stops the coaching and shows Lifeline 13 11 14 and 000.
