How it works

About an hour to set up

You don't write prompts. You show it how you work.

1

Show a session

Upload two or three recorded sessions, or write out how a typical one goes.

2

Answer 20 questions

Your go-to questions, how you open, how you close, what you never say.

3

Correct 10 replies

Read sample replies from your AI and fix them. That becomes its rulebook.

4

Set the limits

What is free, what is paid, and when the AI must hand over to you.

What your AI does

  • Runs practice between your sessions, at any hour.
  • Asks your questions, in your order, one at a time.
  • Ends every chat with one next step.
  • Offers a session with you when someone wants a person.

What it never does

  • Pretend to be you. It is always labelled as an AI.
  • Give therapy or medical advice.
  • Keep coaching through a crisis. It stops and shows help lines.
  • Say anything you have ruled out.

Questions coaches ask

The honest answers

Will the AI replace me?

No. It handles the repetition between sessions: practising an answer, checking in on confidence, one next step. When someone wants a person, it offers a session with you.

What if it says something I wouldn't?

You correct sample replies before it goes live, you can read every conversation afterwards, and you can pause it at any time. Each correction becomes a rule it follows.

Who will I be coaching?

Mostly people aged 17 to 25 who are looking for work or studying: interviews, first jobs, and confidence after rejection.

What does it cost me?

Eluvo takes a small cut of bookings, nothing else. The rate is confirmed with you before your profile goes live.

How are coaches verified?

We check your coaching credentials before your profile is listed. Profiles still being checked are marked "Verification pending".
